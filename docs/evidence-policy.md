# Evidence policy

Status demo: synthetic technical/educational illustration, visibly marked. needs-review: sourced candidate content pending medical review. reviewed: provenance complete and qualified reviewer has checked claims and current applicability. Code cannot establish clinical truth.

Prefer current professional-society guidelines, recognized public-health organizations and peer-reviewed primary research/systematic reviews. Capture source URL, exact organization, version, publication year, evidence category, reviewer/date and scope. Evidence category is descriptive, not a universal grading system; guideline recommendations should retain original grading method in later schema revisions.

Modern-biomedical, traditional-theory and educational-assumption domains are distinct. Traditional roles and syndromes are not proof of mechanisms or efficacy. No unsupported citations, automatic therapy, individual doses or patient prescribing. Safety/red flags precede supplementary content.

Before review promotion: resolve evidence IDs; check provenance/license; validate claims against original sources; check corrections/supersession; record reviewer; label uncertainty; review accessibility and misleading visual encodings. No medical references are invented in Phase 1. Formula documentation states educational assumptions explicitly.
